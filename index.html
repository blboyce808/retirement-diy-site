    What this is

    Retirement.diy is a calculator for planning and education. It produces estimates of how long savings might last under assumptions you choose. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and using it does not make anyone your adviser. Decisions about your money remain yours.

    What it is not

    The figures are the output of a model, not a prediction. They depend entirely on assumptions that may not hold: future returns, inflation, how long you live, and tax law that will change. Real markets do things no model anticipates. Two people with identical inputs can have very different outcomes.

    Some things are deliberately left out, and the help notes say which. Treat a result as one view among several, and check anything important with a qualified professional who knows your circumstances.

    Your data

    Every calculation runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, and there is no account, no login and no analytics. Your figures are kept in your browser storage on this device so the page remembers them; clearing your browser data removes them, and nobody else can read them.

    A scenario code holds your numbers in a scrambled but unencrypted form. Anyone holding a code can read the plan inside it, so share one only with someone you trust. Exported spreadsheets contain your financial data and go wherever you save them.

    The page makes no requests to anyone once it has loaded. The charting library and the heading font are part of the file itself, so no content network, font service or analytics provider sees that you visited, what you looked at, or for how long. You can check this: open your browser’s network tab, or disconnect from the internet and reload. It still works.

    The one exception is the server that sends you the page in the first place, which necessarily sees the request, as it would for any website.
